Frequently Asked Questions About CFA Level 1 Preparation

Who is this CFA Level 1 course for?

This course is designed for anyone preparing for CFA Level 1 — whether you come from a B.Com, Engineering, MBA, or any non-finance background. No prior finance knowledge is required. We teach every concept from first principles.

Do I need a finance background to take this course?

No. The course is built from the ground up assuming zero finance background. Every concept is explained from first principles with real-world examples, so students from any discipline can follow along and succeed.

How long do I have access to the course material?

You get unlimited access with no expiry date. You can rewatch any lecture, re-download notes, and retake mock exams as many times as you need — until you clear the CFA Level 1 exam.

How is this different from coaching institutes that charge Rs 50,000+?

You get the same depth of curriculum coverage — 100+ hours of video lectures, mock exams, PDF notes, and solved problems — at roughly 1/5th the price. Plus, you learn at your own pace, rewatch lectures anytime, and don't need to commute to a classroom.

We recommend 90 minutes per day over a 6-month preparation period. The first 4 months cover the full curriculum (one lecture per day), and the last 2 months are dedicated to mock exams and revision.
