CFA L3, Masters Abroad, & Global Jobs from Pakistan: Your Strategy

Failed CFA Level 3? Want to work abroad from Pakistan? Learn how a Masters, CFA, and strategic job search can pave your way to global finance roles.

Harmeet Hora IIT & IIM Alumni | CFA Charterholder
Mar 2, 2026

Question from a mentee: i m cfa l3 candidate from pakistan. i wasnt able to clear it in first attempt in feb 2024 and wil take it again in next Feb 2025. Currently i also want to switch my job. probably applying for Masters abroad on scholarship bcoz directly getting employment abroad will be tough. u discussed iim jobs but its for indians only.

This is Harmeet Hora, an IIT-IIM alumnus and CFA charterholder, here to provide you with a clear, strategic roadmap. Your situation is not uncommon, and it presents a set of challenges that, with structured effort and the right approach, you can certainly overcome. You are thinking strategically about your career, and that is the first step towards success.

Let us break down your plan and refine it.

Addressing the CFA Level 3 Setback

First, let us address the CFA Level 3 result. Not clearing it in your first attempt is a setback, but it is not a failure. Many highly capable candidates face this. The CFA program, especially Level 3, demands a deep understanding and a specific approach to application.

Here is my honest assessment: You have identified the re-attempt for February 2025. This gives you ample time to prepare rigorously.

My recommendation for your re-preparation:

  1. Perform a Post-Mortem: Do not just re-study everything. You must identify precisely where you fell short. Was it specific topics? Was it time management during the exam? Was it the essay portion? Analyze your performance report from the CFA Institute.
  2. Focus on Weaknesses: Dedicate a disproportionate amount of time to areas where you scored poorly. Level 3 requires mastery across all topics, not just a few.
  3. Practice Essay Questions Extensively: The constructed response (essay) section is unique to Level 3. You must practice writing concise, direct answers that hit the curriculum points. It is not about flowery prose; it is about precision and accuracy.
  4. Maintain Consistency: Structured effort, consistency, and discipline are far more important than sporadic bursts of intense study. Set a daily or weekly study schedule and adhere to it.

Remember, the CFA designation is a global credential that signifies a high level of expertise in investment management. Once you clear Level 3, it will be a powerful differentiator on your resume, especially when combined with a Masters degree. The Strategic Value of a Masters Abroad

Your decision to pursue a Masters abroad, particularly on scholarship, is a sound and pragmatic one, especially given the current job market dynamics for direct international employment from Pakistan.

Why this is a strong move:

  • International Exposure and Network: A Masters program abroad provides you with invaluable international exposure, a global network of peers and professors, and a deeper understanding of global financial markets.
  • Skill Enhancement: You can specialize in areas like Quantitative Finance, Financial Engineering, or Investment Management, which are highly sought after globally.
  • Improved Visa Prospects: Many countries offer post-study work visas, which significantly ease the transition from student to employed professional. Direct employment without a local degree can be much harder due to visa sponsorship requirements.
  • Scholarship Focus: Targeting scholarships demonstrates an excellent understanding of ROI. An education abroad can be expensive, easily costing 20-50 lakhs per year. Securing a scholarship drastically reduces your financial burden and improves your overall career ROI. You should research universities that offer significant scholarships for international students, perhaps through merit-based awards or specific country-focused grants.

When selecting a program, focus on those with strong industry connections, good career services, and alumni networks that are active in global finance hubs. Consider programs in financial centers like London, New York, Singapore, or Dubai.

Combining Your CFA Charter with a Masters Degree

This is where your strategy truly becomes powerful. The combination of a CFA charter and a Masters degree from an internationally recognized university creates a formidable profile.

Here is what each credential brings to the table:

Your Masters degree, on the other hand, provides specialization, academic rigor, and, crucially, a pathway to working in a foreign country. Together, they tell a compelling story about your dedication to finance and your readiness for complex global roles.

Many students from diverse backgrounds, including those from CA or B.Com backgrounds, pursue the CFA to gain this global edge. Navigating Global Job Markets: Beyond “IIM Jobs”

You are correct that platforms like IIMjobs.com are primarily for the Indian market, catering to graduates from premier Indian institutes. My discussions about these platforms are indeed tailored for an Indian audience, as I am an Indian, and my direct experience and network are largely centered here.

However, the principles of job searching are global, and the avenues for international employment are diverse.

Here is how you should approach global job applications:

  1. Leverage Your Masters Program’s Career Services: This is one of the most critical benefits of studying abroad. Universities invest heavily in career support for their international students, including resume building, interview preparation, and connecting you with potential employers.
  2. Master LinkedIn: LinkedIn is your most powerful tool for global job searching.
    • Optimize Your Profile: Ensure your profile highlights your CFA Level 3 candidacy (and soon, charter), your academic achievements, and your career aspirations.
    • Network Proactively: Connect with alumni from your target Masters programs, finance professionals in your desired roles and locations, and recruiters. Engage with relevant content.
    • Apply Directly: Many international firms post roles directly on LinkedIn. Use keywords relevant to your specialization and target locations.
  3. Target International Banks and Investment Firms: Many global firms have offices in various financial hubs. Your combined CFA and Masters profile will make you a competitive candidate for roles in investment banking, asset management, risk management, and research.
  4. Attend Virtual and In-Person Career Fairs: Many universities host career fairs where international employers recruit. Make the most of these opportunities.
  5. Understand Visa Requirements: While your Masters program will provide a pathway, always be aware of the specific visa requirements for post-study work and long-term employment in your target country.

The key is to understand that while direct employment from Pakistan may be challenging due to various factors including visa sponsorship, a Masters degree acts as a crucial bridge. It legitimizes your presence in the target country and provides a structured route to employment.

My Personal Take and Recommendation

Your plan is robust. My recommendation is to execute it with precision and unwavering focus.

  1. Prioritize CFA Level 3: Make clearing Level 3 in February 2025 your immediate and paramount goal. A structured, disciplined approach will be essential. Do not let job searching distract you from this.
  2. Research Masters Programs and Scholarships: Simultaneously, dedicate time to thoroughly research Masters programs that align with your career goals and offer significant scholarship opportunities. Start preparing your applications and essays well in advance. Consider the ROI carefully – a scholarship makes the financial commitment much more palatable.
  3. Integrate Your Story: When you apply for Masters programs and later for jobs, weave a compelling narrative. Explain how your CFA journey has equipped you with analytical prowess, how your past work experience has given you practical insights, and how a Masters will further refine your skills for a global finance career.

This is a long-term strategy, not a quick fix. Success in finance, particularly on a global scale, comes from consistent effort, continuous learning, and strategic decision-making. You are on the right path.
