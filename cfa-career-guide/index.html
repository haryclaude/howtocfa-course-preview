Where Does the CFA Take You?

The CFA charter opens doors across investment management, equity research, portfolio management, risk, corporate finance, and increasingly in fintech and ESG. But the specific path depends on your background, interests, and where you want to be in 5-10 years.

Each guide below covers a different career angle — from specific roles and industries to transition strategies for non-finance professionals.
