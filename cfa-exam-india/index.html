CFA Exam in India: Fees, Centres & Complete Guide

Everything Indian candidates need to know about the CFA Program — exam costs in INR, test centre cities, eligibility, registration, and career prospects after clearing.

CFA Exam Fees in India (INR)

The CFA Institute charges in USD. Below are approximate INR equivalents at ~Rs 83/USD. Actual amounts depend on the exchange rate at the time of payment. GST is not charged by CFA Institute on exam fees (it's a US-based organization), but your bank may levy forex markup (1.5–3.5%).

Fee Component USD Approx. INR Notes
One-Time Enrollment $350 ~Rs 29,050 Paid only once, with Level 1 registration
Exam Fee (Early) $940 ~Rs 78,020 Register early to save — same for all 3 levels
Exam Fee (Standard) $1,250 ~Rs 1,03,750 Late registration costs significantly more
Total (Level 1, Early) $1,290 ~Rs 1,07,070 First-time candidate, early registration
All 3 Levels (Early) $3,170 ~Rs 2,63,110 Total program cost if you pass each level first attempt

Tip: Use a forex-friendly card (like Niyo or Fi) to minimize bank charges on international payments.

CFA Exam Centres in India

CFA exams are conducted at Prometric test centres across India. You choose your preferred city during registration. Centres are available in these major cities:

Mumbai

Multiple centres across Andheri, BKC, and Thane

Delhi NCR

Centres in Gurugram, Noida, and South Delhi

Bangalore

Centres in Koramangala and Whitefield areas

Hyderabad

Centres in Madhapur / HITEC City area

Chennai

Centres in T. Nagar and OMR

Kolkata

Centres in Salt Lake / Sector V

Pune

Centres in Hinjewadi and Kothrud

Ahmedabad

Centres in SG Highway area

Tip: Book your exam slot early — popular cities like Mumbai and Bangalore fill up fast, especially for the February and August windows.

CFA Exam Eligibility in India

You need to meet one of these criteria to register for CFA Level 1:

Bachelor's Degree

Hold a completed bachelor's degree (any discipline — B.Com, B.Tech, BA, BBA, etc.). This is the most common path for Indian candidates.

Final Year Student

Be in the final year of your bachelor's program at the time of registration. You can sit for Level 1 before graduating.

Work Experience

Have 4,000 hours of professional work experience (does not need to be in finance). Accumulated over at least 3 sequential years.

No specific marks percentage or entrance exam is required. CA, CS, MBA, or engineering students are all eligible. There is no upper age limit.

CFA Career Prospects & Salary in India

The CFA charter is widely recognized across India's financial sector. Here's what you can expect:

Role Typical Salary (per annum) Experience
Equity Research Analyst Rs 6–12 LPA 0–3 years
Investment Banking Analyst Rs 8–18 LPA 0–3 years
Portfolio Manager / Fund Manager Rs 15–40 LPA 5–10 years
Risk Manager Rs 10–25 LPA 3–7 years
Wealth Management / Private Banking Rs 8–20 LPA 2–6 years
CFO / Senior Finance Leadership Rs 30–80+ LPA 10+ years

Top employers of CFA charterholders in India include JP Morgan, Goldman Sachs, ICICI Securities, Kotak Mahindra, HDFC AMC, Axis Capital, Edelweiss, Motilal Oswal, and the Big 4 consulting firms.
